Privacy Policy

Space Hamster Run · Last updated 26 November 2026

Space Hamster Run is a small running game made by Oatmeal Morning, a husband-and-wife studio. This page explains what it does and doesn't do with your information. It's short because there isn't much to say.

Nothing is saved about you

The game has no accounts and no server. The only things it remembers, stored on your own device, are your best score in each world, which worlds you've finished, and whether you turned the sound off. On our own test devices it also stores a marker that keeps our test plays out of the statistics. None of this is tied to you, and clearing the app's data removes it.

Anonymous usage statistics

To know whether anyone plays, we count simple events: the game or this website was opened, a store button on this website was tapped, a run was started, ended, won or left part-way, a world was picked, a locked world was tapped, the sound was turned on or off, and the share, feedback or credits button was tapped. Each event carries only the world number and gameplay numbers (score, nuts collected, distance, seconds played, hearts left), plus whether the sound was off and whether the device has a touch screen. Nothing you type and nothing personal is recorded. For this we use PostHog, an analytics service (PostHog Inc., data stored in the United States). PostHog receives the type of device and browser and an approximate location derived from your IP address. This website keeps no identifier between visits, so each visit looks like a new visitor. The app stores one random ID on your phone so we can tell that the same phone played again. It is not linked to your name, your account, your advertising ID or any other app, and clearing the app's data resets it. We do not use cookies, we do not record your screen, and we do not track you across other websites. You can block this with any content blocker; the game and this website work the same without it.

Feedback

The feedback link opens a short form (Google Forms) or your email app. Every question is optional. If you send us feedback, we receive what you write. The form also has an optional box for your email address, in case you'd like a reply; if you fill it in, or write to us by email, we also receive your email address. We use it only to read and answer your feedback. Google hosts the form under its own privacy policy.

Sharing

The share button uses your device's own share sheet (or copies the link) to pass along a link to the page with the store links, and your score. We don't see who you share it with.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log the IP address of visitors. See GitHub's privacy statement.

App stores

If you install Space Hamster Run from the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google may send us anonymous crash reports and basic install statistics under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your name or contact details from them.

What we don't do

We do not sell or share your information. We do not show ads. We never require your name or email, and we do not ask for your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Children

Space Hamster Run is a general-audience game. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. The only personal information we can receive is an email address someone chooses to give us for a reply to their feedback.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
