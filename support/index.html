Space Hamster Run · Run, jump and grab the nuts.

Space Hamster Run is a free running game that plays in your browser, made for phones. Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, we're one email away.

How do I play?

Steer: tap and hold the ◀ ▶ buttons, or drag your finger left and right.

Jump: tap the big JUMP button to hop over obstacles and gaps.

Score: grab the nuts, run as far as you can, and reach the finish of each world.

Hearts: you have five. Bump into an obstacle or fall off the path and you lose one. Grab floating hearts to get them back. Hearts left at the finish give bonus points.

Pause: tap the pause button at the top left. The game also pauses by itself when you switch apps.

On a computer: arrow keys or A/D to steer, Space or ↑ to jump, Esc or P to pause.

How do I unlock the other worlds?

There are five worlds: Space, Ocean, Cave, Sky and Deep Sea. Each one unlocks when you reach the finish of the one before it. Tap any unlocked world on the start screen to fly there, then tap RUN!

Does it save my progress?

Yes, on your own device only: your best score in each world, which worlds you've finished, and your sound setting. There are no accounts, so clearing your browser data (or deleting the app) resets everything.

There's no sound.

Check the speaker button at the top right of the start screen (or SOUND in the pause menu). Phones only play sound after you tap something, so sound starts with your first tap on RUN! If it's still silent, check your phone's silent switch and volume.

The screen is black, or it says it needs 3D graphics.

The game uses 3D graphics (WebGL), which every recent phone and browser supports. Update your browser, or try Chrome, Safari or Firefox. On a computer, make sure hardware acceleration is turned on in your browser's settings.

Can I play it like an app?

Yes. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen, without the browser bar. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android (Chrome): ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen.

How do I contact you?

Email support@oatmealmorning.com. We're a two-person studio, so you'll get a real person, usually within a few days. Bugs, high scores and ideas for new worlds all welcome.
